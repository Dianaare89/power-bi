AdventureWorks Sales Dashboard (Power BI)
Project Overview

In this project, I built an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends using the AdventureWorks dataset.

The goal was to turn raw data into a clear, visual dashboard that helps track performance and support business decisions.

🎯 Business Objectives
Track revenue, profit, and orders
Analyze product and category performance
Understand customer behavior and value
Monitor trends over time

📊 Dashboard Features
🔹 Executive Dashboard
Total Revenue: $24.9M
Total Profit: $10.5M
Total Orders: 25.2K
Return Rate: 2.2%

Includes:

Revenue trend over time
Orders by category
Top-performing products
Monthly KPIs (revenue, orders, returns)

🔹 Product Analysis
Drill-down into individual products
Track orders, revenue, and profit vs target
Price adjustment slider for scenario analysis
Return rate trends

👉 Allows users to interact and test different business scenarios

🔹 Customer Analysis
Total unique customers: 17.4K
Revenue per customer: $1,431
Top 100 customers by revenue
Customer segmentation (income level, occupation)

👉 Helps identify high-value customers and buying patterns

🔹 Regional Analysis
Filters by region (Europe, North America, Pacific)
Geographic view of sales performance
Drill-through to product details

🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI
Data Modeling
DAX (measures & calculations)
Data Visualization

💡 Key Insights
A small number of products drive most revenue
Certain regions perform better than others
Customer segments show different buying behaviors
Revenue and profit trends are increasing over time

💡 What I Learned
How to build interactive dashboards in Power BI
How to create KPIs and DAX measures
How to design dashboards for business users
How to turn data into clear visual insights
